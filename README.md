# Axiom

### Democratizing access to academic research

Axiom is a student-led nonprofit helping high school students learn how research actually works, collaborate with peers and mentors, and share meaningful academic work with the world.

Research opportunities can be difficult to access early. Axiom is built to make that first step easier — whether someone is completely new to research, looking for mentorship, or ready to contribute to a real project.

## What Axiom offers

### Research Foundations

A beginner-friendly course that teaches the fundamentals of academic research, including:

- Research methodology
- Academic writing
- Citations and literature reviews
- Hypothesis design
- Experimentation
- Responsible use of AI
- Peer review
- Publishing and sharing research

The course is self-paced and organized into eight units. Students complete quizzes and a final assessment as they progress.

### Mentored Research

An application-based pathway for students who want to take their ideas further.

Accepted students can work with professors, graduate researchers, and experienced mentors on original research projects over several months.

### Collaborative Research Teams

Students can work across disciplines and countries through peer-led research teams. These teams are designed around accountability, discussion, internal review, and collaborative publishing.

### Open Research Database

Axiom is building a free, searchable archive of peer-reviewed student research.

The goal is to make student work easier to discover and ensure that thoughtful research remains accessible to everyone.

### Volunteer and leadership opportunities

Axiom is built with the help of students and volunteers. Current opportunities include:

- Research reviewer
- Peer mentor
- Student ambassador
- Curriculum developer
- Web developer
- Outreach and marketing
- Research team lead
- Operations and recruitment

Volunteer work can count toward verified service hours, and active contributors can move into leadership roles within the organization.

## How it works

Axiom is designed around a simple progression:

1. **Learn the foundations**
2. **Explore ideas with other students**
3. **Develop and conduct research**
4. **Receive feedback and peer review**
5. **Improve the work**
6. **Publish and share the results**

The emphasis is not just on producing a paper. It is on learning how to ask better questions, evaluate evidence, communicate clearly, and contribute responsibly.

## Why Axiom exists

Many students are interested in research but do not know where to begin. Others face barriers such as limited mentorship, cost, geography, or a lack of access to academic communities.

Axiom exists to help open that door.

The project is guided by five principles:

- **Accessibility**
- **Collaboration**
- **Mentorship**
- **Innovation**
- **Open knowledge**

We believe the next generation of scientists, engineers, economists, writers, and thinkers is already out there. They just need the opportunity to begin.
