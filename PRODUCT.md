# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

High school students exploring research or completing training; prospective volunteers, families and educators learning about Axiom; contributors who manage the site and volunteer program.

## Product Purpose

Give students a clear way to learn how research is done, explore opportunities to collaborate, and take their first steps in responsible academic work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Research Foundations has eight self-paced units, timed assessments and a final assessment. Existing access, identity, progress, referral, CMS and admin behavior stay intact. Describe mentorship and publishing as opportunities, not guarantees; do not represent the in-development archive as a live catalog.

## Brand Commitments

Retain the Axiom name and mark. Use a direct, approachable, academically credible voice. Keep public participation and learning as distinct tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support mobile layouts, keyboard navigation, visible focus and reduced motion. No visual or motion-only control should carry essential information.
